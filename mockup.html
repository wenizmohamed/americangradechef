Sesame-Glazed Buddha Bowl

A vibrant, nutrient-packed bowl featuring roasted vegetables, quinoa, and crispy chickpeas, all brought together with a generous drizzle of American Garden's Roasted Sesame Dressing.

🥘 Ingredients

  • • 1 cup quinoa, cooked
  • • 1 can chickpeas, roasted
  • • 2 cups mixed vegetables (bell peppers, zucchini, carrots)
  • • 2 cups fresh greens (spinach, kale)
  • • 4 tbsp American Garden Roasted Sesame Dressing
  • • Avocado slices and sesame seeds for garnish

📝 Instructions

  1. 1. Roast vegetables at 400°F for 20 minutes until tender and caramelized.
  2. 2. Toss chickpeas with olive oil and spices, then roast until crispy (about 25 minutes).
  3. 3. Assemble bowls with quinoa base, add greens, roasted vegetables, and chickpeas.
  4. 4. Drizzle generously with American Garden Roasted Sesame Dressing.
  5. 5. Top with avocado slices and sprinkle with sesame seeds.

💡 Chef's Fun Fact

Sesame seeds are one of the oldest oilseed crops, with evidence of cultivation dating back over 3,000 years! The nutty flavor in our Roasted Sesame Dressing comes from carefully toasted sesame seeds, bringing ancient tradition to your modern table.
